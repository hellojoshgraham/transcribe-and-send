# Changelog

## 0.4.1

- Restored the original T&S menu-bar indicator. The TX/RX switch now appears for 0.5 seconds after a successful mode-switch shortcut.

## 0.4.0

- Configurable global TX/RX switch, default Command–Option–Shift–S, active in both modes.
- Compact menu-bar switch with active-mode and delivery indicators.
- Shared connection settings, private pairing-code exchange, and safe migration of existing peers.
- Separate Connection, Send, Receive, and Shortcuts & app tabs.
- Explicit build signing configuration, source-only packaging, MIT licence, contribution/security guidance, and macOS CI.

## 0.3.2

- Persistent-certificate build support, clearer permission diagnostics, connection timeout errors, and a receiver status preview.

## 0.3.1

- Menu control for switching between sending and receiving.

## 0.3.0

- Selected local screenshots, authenticated bounded PNG transfer, and image clipboard confirmation.

## 0.2.0

- Press-to-start/stop recording, optional acknowledged automatic paste, and a nonactivating receiver panel.

## 0.1.x

- Initial Superwhisper transcript transport over Tailscale, early local shortcut interception, and explicit recording-folder access.
